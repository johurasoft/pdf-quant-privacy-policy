1. Information Collection and Use

PDF Quant: Editor & Manager is designed to prioritize your privacy by processing your files locally on your device. All core file operations — including PDF compression, merging, splitting, page deletion, image-to-PDF, PDF-to-image, PDF-to-Word, Word-to-PDF, PDF-to-Excel, PDF editing (text, images, shapes, drawing), watermarking, page organizing, PDF locking/unlocking, document scanning and presenting — are performed on your device. We do not upload your files or personal documents to any server. The information the app stores, entirely on your own device, includes: • App settings (theme preference) stored locally via 'shared_preferences' • Recent Documents History: the names and paths of files you create or modify, plus an app-private copy of those files, stored only on your device. This list never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time (see Section 12). • Ad-related identifiers collected by Google AdMob (see Section 4) Document Scanner & Text Recognition (OCR): To scan documents and extract text for Word or Excel files, we use Google ML Kit Document Scanner and Text Recognition. These models run on your device. Your document contents are NEVER uploaded for text extraction. PDF Password Protection: When you use the Lock PDF feature, the password is applied directly to your PDF file on your device. We never see, store, or transmit this password. If you forget it, we cannot recover it. Photo/Image Access: When you use Image to PDF or Edit PDF, the app uses 'image_picker' (Android Photo Picker) to let you select photos. These images are processed on your device and are NEVER uploaded or transmitted by us. We do NOT upload, transmit, or have remote access to: • Your PDF file contents • Contact details, email addresses, or phone numbers • Photos or images beyond what you explicitly select

2. File Processing (Secure On-Device Processing)

All PDF operations — merging, splitting, compressing, editing, scanning, and conversions (Word/Excel/Images) — occur locally on your device: • NO CLOUD UPLOADS: Your files are NEVER uploaded to any server or third-party cloud service. • ON-DEVICE OCR/SCANNING: Google ML Kit processes your documents on your device. Document content is not sent to Google servers. • NO SERVER-SIDE STORAGE: We do not store copies of your documents on any server. Temporary working files are kept in the app's cache and are removed when no longer needed or by Android. A copy of each file you create is kept in the app's private storage so you can reopen it from Recent Documents. 'Remove from list' or 'Clear History' deletes these copies, and uninstalling the app removes everything. • We have NO remote access to the content of your documents at any time.

3. Permissions and Their Purpose

PDF Quant: Editor & Manager only requests permissions essential for its core functionality: • File Selection & Storage: We use the system file picker (Storage Access Framework) via 'file_picker' to let you select PDFs and files, and to save results to your device. On Android 9 and below (API 29 and below), the app may request 'WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE' solely to save files; it is not requested on newer versions. • Image Selection: We use Android's system Photo Picker via 'image_picker'. No broad media permissions are required. • Camera: The camera is used by Google's ML Kit Document Scanner screen when you scan a document. The app itself does not hold the CAMERA permission. • Keep Screen Awake: In the Presenter feature, the app can keep the screen on while a presentation is open. No special permission or personal data is involved. • Internet Access: Required for Google AdMob, the UMP consent form, and for Google ML Kit to download on-device text-recognition models when needed. • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob for ad delivery and measurement. You can reset or opt out via Android Settings → Google → Ads. We do NOT request or use broad 'READ_MEDIA_IMAGES', 'READ_MEDIA_VIDEO', 'READ_MEDIA_AUDIO' or 'MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE' permissions.

4. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect certain data including: • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / AD_ID) • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it, for ad delivery and targeting • Usage data, app interactions and crash/diagnostic logs for ad delivery, measurement and personalization We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent (where legally required) before displaying personalized ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. GDPR & User Consent (UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK, in accordance with GDPR: • Where required by law, a consent dialog is shown when you launch the app • You may Accept or Decline personalized advertisements • Your choice is stored locally and respected immediately • You may change your preference at any time using 'GDPR Privacy Options' in the app's Settings (available where required by region; elsewhere the app shows that it is not required) Declining personalized ads does NOT affect any app functionality. All PDF features work regardless of your ad choice.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services which have their own Privacy Policies: • Google Play Services • Google AdMob — https://policies.google.com/privacy • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) • Google ML Kit (on-device Text Recognition and Document Scanner) • Google Play In-App Review — may be shown to ask for a rating These services may collect information used to identify you as described in their respective policies. Google ML Kit may download its on-device models over the internet when needed; your documents are never uploaded.

7. File Sharing

When you use the 'Share' feature, your selected files are passed directly to third-party applications of your choice (e.g., WhatsApp, Gmail, Bluetooth). Once shared, the data is subject to the privacy policy of the respective application. We have no control over how third-party apps handle your shared data. PDF files that you send to PDF Quant from other apps ('Open with' or 'Share') are processed locally on your device only.

8. Data Storage & Security

• App settings and Recent Documents history (file names, paths and an app-private copy of files you created) are stored locally on your device only, and are never uploaded anywhere • Temporary working files are kept in the app's cache and are removed when no longer needed or by Android. 'Remove from list' or 'Clear History' deletes the Recent Documents copies, and uninstalling the app removes all local data • We do not operate any servers that store your data • No user accounts or passwords are required While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

PDF Quant: Editor & Manager is intended for general audiences and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at the email below so we can take the necessary actions.

10. Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be fully transparent about what we do not collect: • We do NOT sell or rent your personal information to any third party • We do NOT upload or transmit your PDF file contents to any server (file names/paths are kept locally only, as described in Sections 1 and 8) • We do NOT collect contact details, emails, or phone numbers • We do NOT access your device's precise location; only Google AdMob may derive approximate location from your IP address (Section 4) • We do NOT request broad media storage permissions — the app uses system pickers instead • We do NOT share data for advertising purposes beyond AdMob's own policy

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, by Google's services (AdMob, UMP). Google ensures adequate safeguards through Standard Contractual Clauses. For EEA/UK users, your UMP consent covers these transfers. For details: https://policies.google.com/privacy
