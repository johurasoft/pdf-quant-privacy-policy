1. Information Collection and Use

PDF Quant: Editor & Manager is designed to work entirely offline. All file operations — including PDF compression, merging, splitting, page deletion, image-to-PDF conversion, and PDF-to-image conversion — are performed locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or upload your files or personal documents to any server. The information we may use includes: • App settings (theme preference) stored locally via 'shared_preferences' • Ad-related identifiers collected by Google AdMob (see Section 4) Photo/Image Access: When you use the Image to PDF feature, the app uses 'image_picker' to let you select photos from your device gallery. These images are processed entirely on your device and are NEVER uploaded, transmitted, or stored by us. Document Printing: When you use the print feature, the app uses the 'printing' package to send your document to your device's print system. This data is handled by your device's print spooler or connected printer service — not by us. We do NOT collect: • Your PDF file contents or filenames • Location data • Contact details, email addresses, or phone numbers • Photos or images beyond what you explicitly select for conversion

2. File Processing

All PDF operations (merge, split, compress, image to PDF, PDF to image, page delete) occur entirely on your device: • Files are NOT uploaded to any server • Files are NOT stored by us • Temporary files are automatically deleted after processing • We have NO access to your documents at any time

3. Permissions and Third-Party Services

PDF Quant: Editor & Manager requests the following permissions and uses these services: • Image Selection: We use 'image_picker' to allow you to select photos from your gallery or files to convert into PDF. These images are processed locally on your device and are NEVER uploaded to our servers. • PDF Printing: We use a printing service to allow you to preview and print your documents. Note that your document data may be passed through your device's system print spooler or a third-party print application (such as a printer manufacturer's companion app) if configured on your device. We have no control over third-party print applications. • Internet: Required for Google AdMob to display advertisements. • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Automatically used by Google AdMob for ad delivery and measurement. You can reset or opt out via Android Settings → Google → Ads. Note: This app uses system-managed file pickers (Storage Access Framework) to select PDFs and images, which means we do not require or request broad 'Storage' or 'Media' permissions on modern Android devices. All permissions are used strictly for the stated purpose only.

4. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect certain data including: • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / AD_ID) • IP address • Usage data for ad personalization We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before displaying personalized ads, in compliance with GDPR and Google Play policies. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. GDPR & User Consent (UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK, in accordance with GDPR: • On first launch, you will see a consent dialog • You may Accept or Decline personalized advertisements • Your choice is stored locally and respected immediately • You may change your preference at any time from app Settings Declining personalized ads does NOT affect any app functionality. All PDF features work regardless of your ad choice.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services which have their own Privacy Policies: • Google Play Services • Google AdMob — https://policies.google.com/privacy • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) These services may collect information used to identify you as described in their respective policies.

7. File Sharing

When you use the 'Share' feature, your selected files are passed directly to third-party applications of your choice (e.g., WhatsApp, Gmail, Bluetooth). Once shared, the data is subject to the privacy policy of the respective application. We have no control over how third-party apps handle your shared data.

8. Data Storage & Security

• App settings are stored locally on your device only • PDF files are processed in temporary storage and deleted immediately after • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data • We do not operate any servers that store your data • No user accounts or passwords are required While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
